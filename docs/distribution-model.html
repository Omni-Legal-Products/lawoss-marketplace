LAWOSS / DISTRIBUTION MODEL

Your tools. Your deployment.

The Marketplace distributes reviewed plugins. A runtime remains a separate, operator-owned installation — on your computer or on your server.

Updates are not deployments. Updating a plugin does not rebuild or restart your MCP server, change OAuth grants or migrate runtime state. CZ Agents' eight HTTP listeners passed offline token checks; each operator must still verify their own public TLS proxy and source data before deployment.

Catalog model, not a claim that every candidate is released. Fonts use public Google Fonts; offline viewers fall back to local sans-serif, serif and monospace faces.
